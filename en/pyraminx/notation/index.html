Pyraminx notation

Each letter is a tip of the pyramid. The capital letter turns the tip and the layer below it; the lowercase one, just the little tip. The apostrophe (') reverses the direction.

How to hold it

Hold it with one face toward you and one tip up. To scramble, as in WCA competitions, green is in front and yellow on the bottom.

  • The letters are the tips, not the faces: U is the top one, L and R are the two bottom ones in front (left and right), and B is the back one, hidden behind the Pyraminx, on the bottom.
  • Capital or lowercase: U, L, R and B turn the tip and the layer below it; u, l, r and b, just the little tip.
  • The direction: clockwise is as seen looking straight at the tip, as if it pointed at you. The apostrophe (') reverses it.
  • No 2: each turn is 1/3 of a turn (120°). Two turns one way are the same as one the other way (U U is the same as U'), so there is no U2.
