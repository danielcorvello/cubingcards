Last layer

Top-first: step 3

The 3 top edges are left. There are only 5 cases, each with one algorithm: the 5 cards of this step. You look at the front face, find the case and do the algorithm.

First: line up the top

Turn the top (U) until the top tip and the top center match the colors of the faces. The top edges may still be wrong: that's what the cases take care of.

Look at the front face

Look at the 2 top edges of the front face (the two pieces right below the top center) and compare them with the colors of the faces:

  • Both the same color, the right face's: Sune, R U R' U R U R'.
  • Both the same color, the left face's: AntiSune, R U' R' U' R U' R'.
  • The left one with the left color, and the right one with the right color: 2 Flip, R' L R L' U L' U' L.
  • Only the left one with the front color: Lefty Bars, R' U' L' U L R.
  • Only the right one with the front color: Righty Bars, L U R U' R' L'.

If it's none of these, turn the whole Pyraminx (yellow stays on the bottom) so another face is in front and look again: on one of the 3 faces, the case shows up.

After the algorithm, if the top is turned, a U or U' finishes it. Done: the Pyraminx solved.

The cards

The 5 cases of this step are on the L3E cards: the 4 Last Layer ones and 2 Flip. The other 10 cards in the group are for the faster way, the next step.

Cases and algorithms

Each case with its own algorithm, sometimes shorter: to look up, practice and print.
