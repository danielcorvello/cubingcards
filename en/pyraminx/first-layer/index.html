1st layer

Top-first: step 2

Now the 3 bottom edges. Each one has yellow and the color of one face. With them in place, the yellow face is done and each side face gets its bottom row complete.

Find the edge and its place

  1. Pick an edge with yellow. The other color tells you its face: the yellow and green edge goes on the bottom, on the green face.
  2. Turn the whole Pyraminx, always with yellow on the bottom, until that face is in front. The edge's place is on the bottom, in front.
  3. Turn the top (U) until the edge is on top, at the back: the top edge between the left face and the right face.

Bring it down

Look at which way the edge's yellow is facing and do the algorithm for that side:

The two are mirrors of each other. The edges already on the bottom don't come apart: the algorithms always put the bottom pieces back.

If the edge is already on the bottom

If the edge is on the bottom, but flipped (with yellow on the side) or in another edge's place, take it out: hold that place in front and do R U R'. It comes up, and you continue as above.

Do the same with the other two edges. With all 3 on the bottom, only the top layer is left.
