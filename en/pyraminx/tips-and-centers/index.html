Tips and centers

Top-first: step 1

The way to solve the Pyraminx for the first time: by layers, as on the cube. First the tips and the centers, which take a few turns and no algorithm; then the 3 bottom edges; last the 3 top ones. Read it with the Pyraminx in your hand.

The pieces

The Pyraminx has three kinds of piece:

  • Tips (4): the little points. They turn on their own and don't move anything else.
  • Centers (4): right below each tip, with 3 colors. They never leave their place: they only turn, together with the tip above them.
  • Edges (6): between two tips, with 2 colors. These are the ones that take work.

How to hold it

Throughout the method, hold the Pyraminx with one tip up and the yellow face on the bottom. Start with green in front: red is on the left and blue on the right.

The turns are named after the tip, looking at it: U (the top one), L (the left one, in front), R (the right one, in front) and B (the back one). The capital letter turns the tip and the layer below it; the lowercase one (u, l, r, b), just the tip. On its own, the letter is 1/3 of a turn clockwise; with an apostrophe (R'), counterclockwise. All the turns are on the notation page, with a diagram for each one.

Step 1: the tips

Each tip has 3 colors, and so does the center right below it. Turn the tip (with the lowercase letter) until its colors match the center's. A tip has only 3 positions: at most 2 turns.

Do this on all 4 tips. From now on they turn together with the centers and stay right.

Step 2: the centers

A center is right when its colors match the other centers, face by face. To turn a center, use the capital letter: the center turns together with its tip, and the two keep matching.

  1. The 3 bottom centers (those of the L, R and B tips): turn each one until its yellow is on the bottom. The other colors fall into place on their own.
  2. The top center: turn U until its 3 colors match the faces.

Done: the tips and the centers. Each face already has its color on the 3 centers; now the edges are left.
