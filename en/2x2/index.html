Start here

  1. Step 1

    Notation

    Learn the notation: on the 2x2 there are just the face turns (R, U, F…) and the whole-cube rotations.

  2. Step 2

    Solve it with the beginner method

    The 1st layer with the sexy move, then the top one with two algorithms: the Sune and the headlights one, the same as on the 3x3.

  3. Step 3

    Go faster with Ortega

    The white face with the sexy move, then the top one with 7 cases and both layers with 6: the whole 2x2 in 3 steps.

History

  • The 2x2

    The 2x2 came before the Rubik's Cube: American Larry Nichols invented a 2×2×2 held together by magnets in 1970, patented in 1972.
