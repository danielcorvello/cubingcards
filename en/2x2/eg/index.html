CLL / EG intermediateadvanced

Layer and CLL, or face and EG

For those who already use Ortega and want to go faster. In CLL, build the whole bottom layer and solve the top one in one go (40 cases). In EG, the bottom face is enough: the algorithm solves the top layer and fixes the bottom one at the same time (80 more cases, EG-1 and EG-2).
