Reduction intermediate

Centers, edges and like a 3x3, with the parities

Build the centers, pair up the edges and solve it like a 3x3, which is why knowing CFOP helps a lot. The Yau method is the same idea in a different order (two centers, the cross, the other centers and the edges), faster once you get the hang of it.

  1. Centers

    The 6 four-piece centers, in the color order of the 3x3, with the inner layers.

    Learn the step

  2. Edges

    The 12 edge pairs, with a single algorithm: Uw' R U R' F R' F' R Uw.

    Learn the step

  3. Like a 3x3

    With centers and edges done, solve it like a 3x3, turning only the outer faces (R, U, F…): the 3x3 beginner method or CFOP work just the same. Don't turn the inner layers: they break up the centers and the edges.

  4. Parities

    In the 3x3 part, cases that are impossible on a 3x3 may show up. Learn OLL Parity first (a single flipped edge) and then PLL Parity (just two pieces swapped).
