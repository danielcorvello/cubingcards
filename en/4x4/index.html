1 method · 25 cases

Start here

  1. Step 1

    4x4 notation

    Learn the two-layer moves (Rw, Uw…) and the inner-layer ones (2R…). They are what bring the centers and the edges together.

  2. Step 2

    Solve it with reduction

    Build the centers, pair up the edges and solve it like a 3x3, with the parities at the end.

Prefer videos? See the Brazilian channels that teach how to solve the cube.
