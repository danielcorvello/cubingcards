ZZ advanced

ZZ (Zbigniew Zborowski)

  1. EOLine
  2. ZZ F2L
  3. Last layer

Click a step number to mark it as done.

  1. EOLine

    Orient the 12 edges and put the two white edges of the front and back on the bottom (the line). After that, only R, U and L.

    Learn the step

  2. ZZ F2L

    Build a 1×2×3 block on each side of the line, with just R, U and L. The edges already oriented don't flip anymore, so there are no flipped edge cases: it's intuitive, with the pairs going in like in CFOP's intuitive F2L, and your hands stay in place.

  3. Last layer

    Since the edges were oriented at the start, the yellow cross is already done: OLL becomes just the corners (7 cases) and then PLL. To go further, ZBLL solves the whole last layer at once (472 cases). ZZLL and 2GLL are smaller subsets of it, for more specific situations: each card says what needs to be done before.

History of the method

  • ZB and ZBLL

    In 1981, Lars Petrus proposed orienting the edges first, so the last layer could be done in a single step.
