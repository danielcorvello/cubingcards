3x3 notation

Each letter is a turn of one face. The apostrophe (') reverses the direction; the 2 is a half turn. Do each move with the cube in your hand until it comes without thinking.

How to hold it

The letters refer to the cube as it is in your hand, whatever the color: R is always the right face, U the top and F the front. What changes is how you hold it:

  • To scramble, as in WCA competitions: white on top and green in front (red ends up on the right).
  • For the algorithms, as on the cards: yellow on top and green in front (orange ends up on the right). The last layer is on top, in view.

Clockwise is always as seen when looking straight at the face that turns: R looking at the right side of the cube, U looking from above. From the start to the end of an algorithm, the cube stays held the same way; only the whole-cube rotations (x, y and z) change that.

Watch out for the faces you can't see. You hold the cube looking at the front, but clockwise for L, D and B is as seen when looking at them. From the front, they seem to turn the other way:

  • L: the left side goes down (R goes up).
  • D: the bottom layer goes to the right (the top one, in U, goes to the left).
  • B: the top of the back face goes to the left (in F, the top of the front goes to the right).

When in doubt, turn the cube to look at the face, turn it like a clock and put the cube back the way it was.
