ZZLL advanced

The last layer of the ZZ method in one step: edges oriented and the edges already "in phase" (the opposite ones right relative to each other). It's a subset of ZBLL.

BeforeZZ's F2L (edges oriented from the start) with the top edges in phase.
AfterThe solved cube.
