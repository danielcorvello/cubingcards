Before you start

Beginner method: step 1

The beginner method solves the cube in layers: first the bottom one, then the middle one and last the top one. There are 8 short steps, and each one uses at most one or two algorithms. Read this step with the cube in your hand.

The pieces

The cube has three kinds of piece:

  • Centers (6): they have a single color and never leave their place. The center tells you the color of that face: if the front center is green, the front face will end up all green.
  • Edges (12): they sit between two centers and have two colors.
  • Corners (8): they sit at the tips and have three colors.

The colors always come in opposite pairs: white and yellow, green and blue, red and orange. If white is on the bottom, yellow is on top.

A white and green edge has only one right place: between the white center and the green center. A white, green and orange corner goes between those three centers. Solving the cube is taking each piece to its place, with the colors facing the right way.

How to hold the cube

Throughout the method, hold the cube with yellow on top and white on the bottom. Start with green in front. During a step, you turn the whole cube to work on another face, but yellow stays on top.

This is the position of the whole site: the diagrams, the algorithms and the cards start from it. To scramble, the competition rule is different (white on top and green in front). After scrambling, turn the cube so yellow is on top.

How to read an algorithm

Each letter is a face, turned as seen looking straight at it:

  • R is the right, L the left, U the top, D the bottom, F the front and B the back.
  • The letter alone is a quarter turn clockwise, as seen looking at the face that turns.
  • With an apostrophe (R'), it goes counterclockwise.
  • With a 2 (R2), it is a half turn.

Watch out: L, D and B are on the sides you can't see. Their clock is as seen looking at them, so from the front they seem to turn the other way: in L the left side goes down, in D the bottom layer goes to the right. When in doubt, turn the cube to look at the face, turn it and turn the cube back. The notation shows all of them.

The algorithm that shows up most in the method is R U R' U'. It is called the sexy move: the right side goes up, the top turns to the left, the right side goes down and the top comes back. Repeat it a few times until it comes without thinking.

All the moves are on the notation page, with a diagram for each one.

How to use this guide

Tip: the diagrams are examples. Your cube will have other colors out of place, and that's fine: in each step, look only at the pieces of that step (the colored ones in the diagram) and ignore the rest.

Do one step at a time, until you can do it without looking. Then scramble (the site's scrambles help) and solve from the start as far as you know. When every step comes without looking, several times in a row, you have solved the cube on your own.
