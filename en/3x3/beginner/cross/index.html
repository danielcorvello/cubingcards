White cross

Beginner method: step 3

With the daisy done, each petal goes down to its place and forms the white cross on the bottom. The cube stays with yellow on top.

Line up and bring down

Each petal has another color besides white, facing the side. For each one:

  1. Turn the top (U) until that color is above the center of the same color. For example: the green petal above the green center.
  2. Turn that face twice. If it is in front, F2. The petal goes down, with white facing down.

Do the same with the other 3 petals. To work on a petal that is on another side, turn the whole cube around the yellow, without moving anything (the y rotation, in the notation): the face with the lined-up petal becomes the front, and yellow stays on top.

Check

Turn the cube over and look at the white face. The cross has to be complete, and each edge has to match the center beside it (green with green, red with red). If an edge is swapped, take it back to the daisy and bring it down again.

Tip: don't skip lining up. If the petal goes down in the wrong place, the cross ends up with swapped colors and the next step won't work.

If you already make the cross directly, without the daisy, you can use the cards of this step: they show the two most common cases.
