The daisy

Beginner method: step 2

The daisy is the easiest way to start the white cross: you gather the 4 white edges around the yellow center, with white facing up. It looks like a yellow flower with white petals. There is no algorithm here: just a few turns, and you will understand each one.

The diagrams in this step show only the centers and the white edges. The rest of the cube doesn't matter now.

Before each turn: make room

Each petal takes one of the 4 places around the yellow center. Before bringing an edge up, turn the top (U) until the place it will take has no white. That way you don't knock off a petal that was already done.

The white edges, one at a time

Look for a white edge and see where it is.

On the bottom, with white facing down. Turn the face it is on twice. It comes up with white facing up.

In the middle. Turn the face on the side of the white in the direction that takes the edge up. The white arrives facing up.

On top, with white on the side. The edge is in place, but flipped. Turn the front face once (F): it goes down to the middle and you are in the previous case.

On the bottom, with white on the side. Turn the face it is on (F, for example): it goes to the middle, and again it is the middle case.

Repeat until all 4 petals are on top.

Tip: don't think about the order of the petals or their other colors. That is for the next step.
