Beginner method (layers)

The way to solve the cube for the first time: layer by layer, from the bottom up, with yellow always on top. After an introduction page, there are 8 steps, and each one uses at most one or two short algorithms. Open each step with the cube in your hand and follow along with the diagrams. Take your time: here you learn how the pieces move, and that counts for everything else.

History of the method

  • The beginner method (layers)

    Solving in layers (cross, 1st layer corners, middle edges and the last layer in a few steps) is the oldest idea: the first known publication is by Donald Taylor, in 1978.
