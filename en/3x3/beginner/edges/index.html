Edges in place

Beginner method: step 9

The last step. The corners are right and the top face is yellow; what's left are the top edges, which cycle among themselves until each one reaches its place.

Look for a solved edge

Look at the 4 sides of the top layer. An edge is right when its side color matches the corners on that side: the side shows a full stripe of a single color.

The algorithm

R2 U R U R' U' R' U' R' U R'

It cycles the three edges at the front, left and right, and leaves the back one in place.

One solved edge: turn the whole cube so it is at the back. Do the algorithm. If the edges cycled the wrong way, do it again: it takes at most two times.

The arrows show where the three edges need to go. If it is the direction of the algorithm, once is enough; in the opposite direction, it takes two.

No solved edge: do the algorithm from any side. A solved edge shows up, and you do as above.

Congratulations!

You solved the Rubik's Cube.

To lock it in: scramble (you can use the site's scrambles) and solve it about 10 times following these pages, one step at a time. Then try without looking, going back only to the step where you get stuck. To practice a single step, use the Train button on its page.

After the beginner method

When the beginner method gets easy, next is CFOP. It uses the same cross, joins the 1st and 2nd layers into a single step (F2L) and does the last layer in two steps, with 2-Look OLL and 2-Look PLL. The yellow cross and yellow face of this method are already 2-Look OLL.
