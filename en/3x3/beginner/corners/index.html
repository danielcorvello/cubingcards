Corners in place

Beginner method: step 8

The top face is yellow, but the corners may be swapped among themselves. Now each corner goes to its place, looking at the side colors. The top edges may move: they are for the last step.

Look for the headlights

Look at the sides of the top layer. When the two corners on one side have the same color, like the two headlights of a car, those two corners are right relative to each other.

The algorithm

R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'

It swaps the two right corners (and two edges, which don't matter now). Break it into three parts to memorize: the sexy move R U R' U', then R' F R2 U' R', and finally U' R U R' F'.

With headlights: turn the top (U) until the headlights are on the left. Do the algorithm.

No headlights (no side with two matching corners): do the algorithm from any side. The headlights show up, and you do it again with them on the left.

Line up the top

At the end, all 4 corners are right relative to each other. Turn the top (U) until each corner is above the centers of its colors.

Cases and algorithms

Each case with its own algorithm, sometimes shorter: to look up, practice and print.
