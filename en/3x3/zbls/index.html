ZBLS advanced

ZB Last Slot: inserts the last F2L pair and, at the same time, orients the top edges (forms the yellow cross). Together with ZBLL, it's the ZB method.

BeforeThree F2L pairs and the last pair in any position.
AfterZBLL (the whole last layer with the edges oriented).

Used in:CFOP
