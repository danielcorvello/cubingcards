  1. Cross

    In CFOP, the cross is built directly on the bottom (without making it on top and flipping the cube). Use the 15 seconds of inspection to plan the whole cross before you start; with time, you can plan the first pair too. The cross trainer practices this.

    Learn the stepTrain

  2. F2L

    Once you understand intuitive F2L, learn the algorithms for the cases where your own solution gets long: the 41 F2L cases, with the pair in the front right slot. You don't need to memorize them all at once: start with the ones you get wrong the most.

    Most of the time in CFOP goes into F2L. Practice lookahead (looking for the next pair without stopping), use the other slots without rotating the cube (the F2L other slots group) and learn the Advanced F2L cases for the hard situations.

  3. OLL

    The last layer in four steps: in 2-Look OLL, the yellow cross (OLL 45, 44 and 2) and the corners (OLL 21 to 27); in 2-Look PLL, the corners (T and Y) and the edges (H, Ua, Ub and Z). That's 16 algorithms, and you already know many of them from the beginner method.

    Then, the 57 OLLs. Go by families (the ones with a similar shape together) and mark the status of each case on the OLL page as you learn. With full OLL and PLL, the last layer takes two looks.

  4. PLL

    Replace 2-Look PLL with the 21 PLLs, one case at a time. In general it pays to start with PLL: there are fewer cases and they show up in almost every solve. Use the 2-sided PLL trainer to recognize the case looking at only two faces. An order that works well, one case at a time: one A and one U; the other U; E; H and Z; the other A; T; F; the Js; the Ns; Y; V; the Rs; and last the Gs.

  5. Last slot

    Optional, after OLL and PLL: algorithms that insert the last F2L pair and, at the same time, get a head start on the last layer. Each group is for one situation:

    • WV and SV: with the yellow cross done and the last pair on top, they insert the pair and orient the corners. The whole OLL is done: only PLL is left.
    • VLS: the same, without needing the yellow cross; it orients the whole last layer.
    • CLS: with the edge of the last pair already in place, it inserts the corner and orients the top corners.
    • FRUF: for when the 3 pairs and the cross end up done with the front face turned.
    • ZBLS: inserts the last pair and orients the top edges, from any position. It is the way into ZBLL.

    Start with WV (27 cases): it shows up whenever the last pair comes out built on top.

  6. Advanced last layer

    Only after all of that is solid: COLL (corners when the yellow cross already came), WV and VLS (orienting on the last pair), OLLCP, and finally ZBLL, Anti PLL and ELL. There are hundreds of cases; pick one, learn it bit by bit and open each group to see what it does.

History of the method

  • CFOP (Fridrich)

    CFOP = Cross, F2L, OLL, PLL.

  • ZB and ZBLL

    In 1981, Lars Petrus proposed orienting the edges first, so the last layer could be done in a single step.

  • Last slot: WV, SV and VLS

    Ideas for orienting the last layer while inserting the last pair, skipping OLL.

  • OLLCP

    Doing OLL while also putting the corners in place was proposed by Masayuki Akimoto and others in the early 2000s (first known mention in 2005).
