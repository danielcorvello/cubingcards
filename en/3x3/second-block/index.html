2nd block (Roux)

Roux: step 2

The 2nd block is the mirror of the 1st: a 1×2×3 block on the right side, at the same height. The rule is not to touch the 1st block, so only R, r (Rw), U and M are allowed. The middle slice (M) is free: you can turn it as much as you like.

1. The bottom edge

First, the bottom right edge (the white and orange one, with white on the bottom and green in front), next to the right center. Use M and r to bring the edge and R to insert it.

2. The back square

Then, a pair (corner and edge) at the back, forming a 1×2×2 square with the bottom edge. The pair forms on top, like in intuitive F2L, and goes in with R, r and U. Think of r as an R that takes the middle slice along: it doesn't ruin anything, because the middle is still free.

3. The last pair

Finally, the front pair, the same way. It's Roux's "last slot": most come out intuitively, and the annoying cases have algorithms on the cards of this step (SBLS).

With both blocks done, the 4 top corners (CMLL) and the 6 middle and top edges (LSE) are left.

Cases and algorithms

Each case with its own algorithm, sometimes shorter: to look up, practice and print.
