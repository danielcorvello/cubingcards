Skewb notation

The Skewb turns around its corners: each turn moves half of it (a corner, its 3 neighbouring corners and 3 centers), a third of a turn. The apostrophe (') reverses the direction.

How to hold it

Hold it with the 1st layer on the bottom and yellow on top, as in the algorithms on the cards.

  • The letters are the top corners: R is the front right one, F the front left, L the back left and B the back right. The turn moves the half of the Skewb that contains the corner.
  • The direction: clockwise is looking at the corner from outside, as if it pointed at you. The apostrophe (') reverses it.
  • No 2: each turn is a third of a turn (120°). Two turns one way are the same as one the other way (R R is the same as R').
  • The two triggers: the sledgehammer is R' F R F'; the hedgeslammer, F R' F' R. Almost every algorithm in Sarah's method is made of them.

Scrambles use another notation, the WCA one: with white on top and green in front, R, U, L and B turn around 4 corners that never move (R: bottom back right; U: top back left; L: bottom front left; B: bottom back left).
