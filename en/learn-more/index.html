Learn more

Prefer to learn by watching? These channels teach how to solve the cube, from the first solve to advanced methods. Plus the community, competitions and references to go further.

Featured channels

  • J Perm

    One of the best-known cubing teachers in English. Covers beginner to advanced CFOP, 2x2 (including Ortega), 4x4 with the Yau method, blindfolded and fewest moves.

    • Beginner
    • CFOP
    • 2x2
    • 4x4
    • Blindfolded
    Visit the channel

  • Z3Cubing

    Entertaining, informative videos about the Rubik's Cube and many other puzzles. Has a full CFOP tutorial and a recent series of tutorials for 2x2, Pyraminx and Clock.

    • CFOP
    • 2x2
    • Pyraminx
    • Other puzzles
    Visit the channel

  • Cubeorithms

    Nolan ("Blue") combines cubing and animation. His tutorials include how to solve the Megaminx, a Megaminx last layer guide and the Kilominx.

    • Beginner
    • Megaminx
    • Other puzzles
    Visit the channel

More channels

  • CubeSkills

    Feliks Zemdegs' tutorial channel. Has an advanced F2L series, 4x4 and 5x5 tips, and one-handed PLL algorithms.

  • Kian Mansour

    The go-to English channel for the Roux method: advanced Roux techniques, a full EOLR tutorial, one-handed Roux and the Meyer method for 4x4.

  • TheCubicle

    The official channel of the TheCubicle.com store. Besides cube reviews, it has how-to videos with top cubers, like F2L tips and skill-level series on other puzzles.

Community and competitions

  • SpeedSolving.com Forum

    The oldest English speedcubing forum, started in 2006, with a beginner FAQ and threads on methods, hardware and competitions.

  • r/Cubers

    The main cubing community on Reddit, with daily discussion threads, solve critiques, puzzle recommendations and competition news.

  • WCA Competitions

    The official calendar of World Cube Association competitions, where you can find an event near you.

Going further

  • World Cube Association

    The body that runs official competitions, with the rules, results, rankings and records.

  • SpeedSolving Wiki

    A community encyclopedia of methods, algorithm sets and terms, with a beginner's introduction to speedcubing.

  • jperm.net

    J Perm's site with written tutorials for 3x3, 2x2, 4x4, one-handed and blindfolded, plus algorithm sheets and trainers for OLL, PLL, COLL, Winter Variation, 2x2 CLL/EG-1 and 4x4 parity.

  • CubeSkills

    Feliks Zemdegs' tutorial site with video series and algorithm pages for 3x3, one-handed, 4x4, 5x5, bigger cubes and Megaminx.

  • SpeedCubeDB

    A huge database of algorithms for every case, across many puzzles.

  • CubingApp

    Algorithm pages for 3x3, 2x2, Pyraminx, Skewb and Square-1, plus rankings and a competition finder.

  • Sarah's Skewb method

    Sarah Strong's own page about her method: beginner, Intermediate and Advanced.

  • Skewb Trainer (cuberoot)

    Practice the first layer and the L2L cases of the Skewb.

  • Tao Yu's Alg Trainer

    Practice algorithms with a virtual cube, scrambles and a timer, with dozens of sets put together by the community.

  • csTimer

    A free browser timer with official-style scrambles for every puzzle, session statistics and training tools.

  • Ruwix

    A wiki of Rubik's Cube and twisty puzzle solutions, with beginner guides for many puzzles.

Know a good channel or site that is not here? Suggest it in a GitHub issue (the link is in the footer).
