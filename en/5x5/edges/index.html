Edges

Reduction: step 2

With the centers done, join the 3 pieces of each edge: the middle piece and the two wings. The algorithm is the same as on the 4x4, Uw' R U R' F R' F' R Uw, and it joins all 3 at once.

Find the 3 pieces of an edge

Look for the middle piece of an edge (green and orange, for example) and the two wings with the same colors.

Set up the position

Use only the outer faces (R, U, F…), which don't break up the centers or the finished edges:

  1. The middle piece goes in front, on the right, in the middle band.
  2. The two wings go in front, at the same height (both on top), one on the left and the other on the right.
  3. The two wings show in front the color the middle piece doesn't show. If the middle shows orange in front, the wings show green.

Do the algorithm

Uw' joins the 3 pieces on the right, R U R' F R' F' R stores the finished edge and brings another one to its place, and Uw puts the centers back.

With the two wings on the bottom, do the same from below: Dw R U R' F R' F' R Dw'.

The last two

Join 10 edges this way. The last 2 have their own cases: the cards of this step (L2E). After that, the cube is a big 3x3.

Cases and algorithms

Each case with its own algorithm, sometimes shorter: to look up, practice and print.
