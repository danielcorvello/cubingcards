5x5 Cube

1 method · 30 cases

See the methodsTrain

Start here

  1. Step 1

    5x5 notation

    The same moves as the 4x4, plus the middle layer (M, E, S) and 3Rw.

  2. Step 2

    Solve it with reduction

    Build the centers, join the edges and solve it like a 3x3: on the 5x5, with no parity at the end.
