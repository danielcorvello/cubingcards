  1. Centers

    Build the centers (9 pieces each). The first four are intuitive; the last 2 have cases (L2C).

    Cases and algorithms

  2. Edges

    Join the 3 pieces of each edge; the last 2 have cases (L2E). Then, solve it like a 3x3: on the 5x5 there is no parity in that part.

    Learn the stepTrain

    Cases and algorithms

    • L2E
      0/13

  3. Like a 3x3

    With centers and edges done, solve it like a 3x3, turning only the outer faces (R, U, F…): the 3x3 beginner method or CFOP work just the same. Don't turn the inner layers: they break up the centers and the edges.
