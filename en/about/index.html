About CubingCards

CubingCards makes study cards (flashcards) for speedcubers: pick the cases, print, cut and practice. It also explains the methods step by step, has several algorithms for each case (and you can add your own) and training tools.

Everything on the site is free and works without sign-up. What you mark (status, chosen algorithms, times) is stored in your browser. To take it to another device, use "Export my data" in My progress.

There are 2,913 cases and 23,016 algorithms in the catalog today.

How it started

CubingCards was born in a group of friends learning to solve the cube together. We kept trading links to sites and videos, each one with their own favorites, their own algorithms and their own printed cards. So came the idea of putting it all in one place, in Portuguese: the algorithms, the explanations, the cards to print and the tools to practice.

Behind the scenes

I am Daniel Corvello and I vibe coded this site in my spare time, between one solve and the next, with the help of Claude. I am not an experienced cuber: I am learning and made the site to gather the algorithms. There may be a crooked algorithm, a case image drawn wrong, a typo or a button that does not work. Found one? Great: you just became part of CubingCards. Tell us in an issue (with your suggestion, if you have one) and the next cuber will find it all fixed.

Where the algorithms come from

The catalog algorithms were gathered bit by bit from sites, spreadsheets, videos and books the cubing community shares, and from our own notes. Thanks to everyone who collects, tests and publishes algorithms: without that work, this site would not exist.

Libraries

The case images are drawn with sr-visualizer (LGPL-3.0) and svg.js (MIT). The 3D animation uses cubing.js (MPL-2.0 or GPL-3.0).
