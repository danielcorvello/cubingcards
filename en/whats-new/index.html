What's new

What came to CubingCards in each release, newest first.

  1. v0.1 · build 16Nov 23, 2026

    • NewNew puzzle: the Skewb. It has the notation, Sarah's method (Intermediate, 10 cases, and Advanced, 93 cases), cards, training, timer and WCA scrambles. The 1st layer and the centers have guided pages, with pictures and algorithms.
    • ImprovedOn the home page, the 7 puzzles sit in two rows, 4 on top and 3 centered below.
    • ImprovedWhen printing, the chosen blocks are listed under "Continue", and the chosen card gets a colored background and a ✓.
    • ImprovedNew glossary terms: sledgehammer, hedgeslammer, Pi and Peanut, L2L and Sarah's method. "Learn more" has two Skewb references.

  2. v0.1 · build 15Nov 22, 2026

    • NewOn the method pages, each step shows a picture of the puzzle as it looks after that step: what is done in color, the rest in gray. This covers every method on all 6 puzzles.

  3. v0.1 · build 14Nov 22, 2026

    • ImprovedEach puzzle's page shows the puzzle's picture next to the title, and the cards line up in 4 columns.
    • ImprovedNew glossary terms: Top-first, Varasano, Star and Wide move. EO now also covers the last layer (the yellow cross and the Megaminx star).
    • ImprovedIn the notation pages, the 4x4 now has Uw2 and 2R2, the 3x3 explains U2' (a half turn the other way) and the Megaminx texts are clearer.
    • ImprovedIn the triggers, Bigger-U Unsexy notes that it differs from Bigger-U Sexy but shows the same numbers, because sources almost never write U2'.

  4. v0.1 · build 13Nov 22, 2026

    • NewHow-to page for the 4x4 parities: how to spot OLL Parity and PLL Parity, with one algorithm for each and a picture of the case.
    • NewHow-to page for the Megaminx last layer in 4 stages (star, yellow face, edges and corners), with the easiest cases of each.
    • ImprovedIn the 4x4, 5x5 and Megaminx case animations, the pieces that don't matter are gray, as on the 3x3 and 2x2.
    • ImprovedThe 2x2, 4x4, 5x5 and Megaminx methods now have a description line on their card and, some, an opening paragraph.
    • ImprovedShorter names on the Megaminx and Pyraminx, without the repeated "Megaminx" and "Pyra".
    • Improved4x4 and 5x5 algorithms use the WCA notation for wide moves (Rw, Uw…), with no duplicates.
    • ImprovedIn practice mode, the recognition text matches the chosen puzzle, and the first time starts on the method's first group.
    • FixedThe Megaminx case animations now show yellow on top, matching the pictures.
    • FixedWhen printing Guimond for the 2x2, the cards come out in the method's order.
    • FixedIn 5x5 L2C, the case numbering no longer skips 15.

  5. v0.1 · build 12Nov 18, 2026

    • NewBeginner method for the 2x2: solve it with two algorithms, the Sune and the T-perm (the same as on the 3x3), with step-by-step pages and cards for the yellow face and the corners.
    • NewThe Guimond method on the 2x2: how-to pages for each step (¾ of a face, orientation and separation), the 16 orientation cases and the 6 separation cases, and the method's history.
    • NewGuimond with 2 algorithms: a version to get started, with just R' F U' R and the A-perm.
    • ImprovedOn the 2x2 how-to pages, clicking a picture opens the cube in 3D, and the move sequences play as an animation.
    • ImprovedIn the 2x2 case animations, the colors that don't matter are dimmed, as on the 3x3: in Ortega OLL, for example, only the top yellow shows.

  6. v0.1 · build 11Nov 17, 2026

    • NewIn the notation practice, rounds of 10, 20 or 30 questions. At the end you see the score, the time, your best round and the moves you missed, each with its drawing.
    • NewIn the notation practice, pick which moves are included: faces, slices, wide moves, rotations and, depending on the puzzle, the Pyraminx tips or the Megaminx R++ and D--.
    • ImprovedMy progress shows the notation quiz for each puzzle: the rounds, the best one and the moves you miss the most.
    • ImprovedIn scrambles, the .txt file name includes the puzzle (cubingcards-scrambles-3x3-…), and printed sheets show "cubingcards.com.br", small, at the bottom.
    • FixedA chosen dark theme no longer switches back to light on its own when opening some pages.

  7. v0.1 · build 10Nov 16, 2026

    • NewOn each case page, "Same case in other groups": the Sune (OLL 27), for example, also shows up in COLL and ZBLL, with a link to each.
    • NewA "What is it?" icon next to the title of methods and groups: hover to read the explanation or click to open the glossary.
    • ImprovedThe glossary gained 18 terms, such as CLS, WV, VLS, ZBLS, 2GLL, ZZLL, Anti PLL and Pure OLL.
    • ImprovedPrinted cards now show "cubingcards.com.br", small, at the bottom of the back. To remove it, turn it off in Advanced options.
    • ImprovedIn About, how CubingCards started, with screenshots of the first version. In Learn more, new references: SpeedCubeDB, CubingApp and Tao Yu's Alg Trainer.

  8. v0.1 · build 9Nov 15, 2026

    • ImprovedWhen creating cards, the panel shows only the options that apply to what you picked: with just the notation, for example, the alternatives on the back, the scramble and the cover cards are hidden.

  9. v0.1 · build 8Nov 15, 2026

    • NewThe site is now also in English, at cubingcards.com.br/en: algorithms, methods, notation, history, glossary, search and printing. Switch the language in the footer or in Preferences.
    • ImprovedThe button to build your cards is now called "Create cards".
    • ImprovedScrambles: download as .txt, one per line, and pick from 1 to 5 sheets. While they are being generated, the screen lets you know.
    • FixedThe preview of the card, calibration and scramble sheets fits the screen at any width, without cutting off the sides.
    • FixedIn the case grid, hovering over the status no longer covers the cell border.

  10. v0.1 · build 7Nov 12, 2026

    • NewThe beginner's method diagrams open the cube in 3D: the ones that show turns play the animation, and the still ones can be rotated to see every side.
    • ImprovedBeginner's method revised from start to finish, with more diagrams, examples with the colors in view, the missing cases and what to do when the cube was put together wrong.
    • ImprovedThe turn arrows became a ring around the face that turns, and the move appears between the before and after diagrams.
    • ImprovedThe notation explains the clockwise direction of L, D and B, which seem to turn the other way when seen from the front.
    • ImprovedOn the method page, each step has "Learn the step" and "Train" buttons, and the cases and algorithms appear in smaller cards.
    • ImprovedOn the set page, the button says where it leads, like "How to do it: Intuitive F2L".
    • ImprovedAlgorithms stand out in the texts.

  11. v0.1 · build 6Nov 10, 2026

    • NewPreferences page, with theme, triggers in the algorithms, your printer, privacy and deleting your data. Open it from the gear in the top bar.
    • ImprovedMy progress shows at the top what is in progress and your training times. The sections open and close, and your choice is remembered.
    • ImprovedProgress bars in three colors (learned, learning and the rest), with the short number next to them.
    • ImprovedThe level on set cards shows as small bars, taking up less space.
    • ImprovedOn the print page, the printer and paper options are together in "My printer".
    • ImprovedOn the set page, the case types (Adj Swap, Opp Swap, EPLL) explain what they mean when you hover over them.
    • ImprovedOn phones, "Pick up where you left off" appears at the top of the home page.
    • ImprovedExporting your data shows the name of the downloaded file.
    • Fixed"Pick up where you left off" suggests the next cases in the same order as the set page.
    • FixedThe calibration background test no longer shows extra empty boxes outside the sheet.
    • FixedThe background chosen for the cards is no longer lost when changing other options with the Side band or Clean style.

  12. v0.1 · build 5Nov 10, 2026

    • New"What's new" page, with what goes into each release.

  13. v0.1 · build 4Nov 10, 2026

    • ImprovedThe top bar and the footer fit better on tablets and medium-sized windows.
    • ImprovedThe footer now has two columns: "The cube" and "The site".
    • Fixed"Pick a method" on the 3x3 page led to a page that didn't exist.
    • Improved"About" explains how to take your progress to another device.

  14. v0.1 · first releaseNov 9, 2026

    • NewStudy cards to print, cut out and practice with: 2x2, 3x3, 4x4, 5x5, Megaminx and Pyraminx.
    • NewThe methods explained step by step: the beginner's method, CFOP, Roux, ZZ and more.
    • NewThousands of cases, with algorithms from several sources so you can pick yours.
    • NewTraining: timed solves, case timer, recognition, 2-sided PLL, cross and notation.
    • NewScramble analysis and scramble lists.
    • NewMy progress, saved in your browser, with export and import.
    • NewSearch, glossary and the history of the methods.
