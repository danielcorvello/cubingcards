Star and 1st face

Layers: step 1

The Megaminx has 12 faces, but it is solved like the 3x3: one face first, then the layers around it, and the top one at the end. Here white goes on the bottom and yellow on top, as on the 3x3. The faces have their own names: around the top one, F (the front one), R, L and the back ones; below, between two top faces, DR (the front right one) and DL (the front left one).

The star

First, the 5 white edges around the white center: the "star", like the 3x3 cross. Each edge has another color, which has to match the center of the neighboring face.

It comes without an algorithm. Look for a white edge, turn the top faces until it is above its place and bring it down by turning the bottom face on that side (DR, DL…). The ones already in stay on the bottom and don't move.

The corners: the sexy move

Now come the 5 white corners, with the Megaminx sexy move: DR F DR' F'. It's the same idea as the 3x3's R U R' U'.

  1. Hold it with white on the bottom and the empty spot in front, on the bottom: between DR and DL.
  2. Turn the top faces until the corner is at the bottom tip of the front face, right above the spot.
  3. Repeat the sexy move until the corner goes down with white facing down.

You don't need to count: do the sexy move and look. The star and the other corners stay in place, because the sexy move only moves the front bottom corner. If a corner is already on the bottom, but wrong, hold it in that spot and do one sexy move to bring it up.
