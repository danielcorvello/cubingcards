Megaminx notation

The Megaminx has two notations. In the algorithms, each letter is a turn of one face: 1/5 of a turn (72°), the apostrophe (') reverses the direction and the 2 is 2/5 of a turn. In WCA scrambles, R++, R--, D++ and D-- turn almost the whole Megaminx at once.

How to hold it

  • To scramble, as in WCA competitions: white on top and green in front. The R++ and R-- turns take everything except the left part 2/5 of a turn (144°) one way or the other; D++ and D--, everything except the top part. The U closes each line of the scramble.
  • For the algorithms, U is the top face, the one you are solving, and F the front one. The other letters (R, L, BR, BL, DR, DL…) are the faces around it, by their position.

Clockwise is always as seen when looking straight at the face that turns. From the start to the end of an algorithm, the Megaminx stays held the same way.
