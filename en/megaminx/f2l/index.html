Middle layers

Layers: step 2

With the white face done, build the layers around it, from the bottom up, until only the top face is left. There are 4 steps, and each one uses a short algorithm, similar to the 3x3 ones. In all of them, the algorithm only moves the piece going in: what's already done stays done.

1. The edges between the bottom faces

The 5 edges between two bottom faces (between DR and DL, for example), above the white corners. Hold it with the empty spot in front, on the bottom, and take the edge to one of the bottom borders of the front face, turning the top faces. It goes in one of these two ways:

If the edge is in place, but flipped, do one of the two to take it out and put it in again.

2. The 3rd layer corners: another sexy move

The 5 corners right above those edges, at the bottom tip of each top face. The sexy move is now R' F' R F: take the corner to the bottom right tip of the front face (between F, R and DR) and repeat until it goes in.

3. The 3rd layer edges

4. The 4th layer

Last, the 5 corners between two top faces and a bottom one, and the 5 edges between two top faces. It's just like the 3x3's 1st and 2nd layers. The corners go in with the 3x3 sexy move, R U R' U', from the top layer:

And the edges, with the 3x3 2nd layer algorithms, starting from the edge on top, in front, with the front color facing front:

Done: only the top face is left, with the last layer cards.
