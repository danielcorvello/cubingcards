Layers beginneradvanced

Star, middle layers and the last one in 4 stages

The way to solve the Megaminx for the first time: like the 3x3 beginner method, face by face starting from the white one, until only the top is left. The last layer takes 4 stages, with few algorithms in each; to go further, the full OLL and PLL.

  1. Star and 1st face
  2. Middle layers
  3. Last layer in 4 stages
  4. OLL and PLL

Click a step number to mark it as done.

  1. Star and 1st face

    The "star" (the 5 edges of the white face, like the cross) and the 5 white corners, with the sexy move DR F DR' F'.

    Learn the step

  2. Middle layers

    The layers around the white face, from the bottom up, in 4 steps with short algorithms, until only the top face is left.

    Learn the step

  3. Last layer in 4 stages

    EO (the top star), CO (the top face one color), EP and CP (the pieces in place). Few algorithms per stage.
